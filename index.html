Research

Published · Information & Management

The Goldilocks Question: How Optimal Length and Answerer Match Boost Reader Payments on Paid Q&A Platforms

With Huigang Liang. Using 17,351 observations from Weibo Q&A, the study finds an inverted U-shaped effect of question length on paying readers and shows that answerer–question match strengthens this relationship.

Major Revision · MIS Quarterly

Pandora’s Box Opened: A Global Analysis of ChatGPT’s Impact on AI Incidents

With Huigang Liang and Yajiong Xue. A country-level panel study of 126 countries examining whether ChatGPT access increased AI incidents and how AI investment and institutional quality condition that effect.

Major Revision · MIS Quarterly

Why Some Countries Are Hit Harder: Cultural Tightness and the Global Distribution of DDoS Attacks

With Huigang Liang and Yajiong Xue. Using daily DDoS data across 57 countries, the study examines population, national cybersecurity capability, and cultural tightness as joint drivers of national cyber risk.

Manuscript in preparation

Does Belief Systems Matter? The Moderation Role of Hofstede’s Cultural Dimensions on National DDoS Attack

Extends my national cybersecurity research by examining how multiple cultural dimensions shape cross-country DDoS attack intensity.

Data collection in progress

When Algorithms Speak Differently: How Training Data Shapes the Social Influence of AI Comment Robots

Examines how training data can shape the social influence of AI agents and the dynamics of human–AI interaction.
